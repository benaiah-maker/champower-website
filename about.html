WHO WE ARE

Delivering Reliable Energy Solutions for a Changing World

CHAMPOWER LTD is a Kenyan renewable energy company dedicated to making clean and reliable energy accessible to homes, businesses, farms, institutions, and industries.

Established in 2014, the company has built its operations around providing quality solar products, energy solutions, and technical support to meet the diverse energy needs of its customers.

From solar panels and batteries to inverters, lighting systems, and other renewable energy solutions, CHAMPOWER LTD works to provide practical energy solutions that support both everyday needs and long-term sustainability.
